1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites work efficiently and provide information to site owners. Similar technologies — web beacons, pixels, and local storage — are also covered by this policy.

2. Websites This Policy Covers

This Cookie Policy applies to the Axerio Automation main website (axerioautomation.com) and wedding websites built and hosted by Axerio Automation on behalf of our clients.

3.3 Third-Party Cookies

Third-party services embedded in our websites may set their own cookies. We do not control these cookies.

  • Google Maps (wedding websites with a Location section) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Formspree (RSVP and music request forms) — Formspree Privacy Policy
  • YouTube / Vimeo (video embeds on Prestige/Elite tier sites) — may set cookies even before you play the video.

4. Cookies We Do NOT Use

  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies for targeted advertising.
  • We do not use cookies to build personal profiles for marketing.
  • We do not sell cookie data to third parties.

5. Managing Your Cookies

5.1 Browser Settings

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: chrome://settings/cookies
  • Firefox: about:preferences#privacy
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy
  • Edge: edge://settings/cookies

Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent parts of the website from functioning correctly.

5.2 Opt-Out Tools

  • Google Analytics opt-out
  • Google Ads Settings
  • Your Online Choices (EU)
