1. Introduction

Welcome to Axerio Automation ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you interact with our services — including our wedding website platform, our AI automation services, and any associated websites or platforms we operate (collectively, the "Services").

Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with any part of it, please discontinue use of our Services.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide Directly

As a client: Full name, email address, phone number, business name, payment information (processed via third-party processors — we never store card details), and project details.

As a wedding website guest (visiting a client's site): Name, email address, number of guests, meal preferences, song requests, guestbook messages, and other information submitted via forms.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our websites, we may automatically collect IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and session timestamps via server logs and analytics tools.

3.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Google Maps — embedded maps may collect data under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Formspree — RSVP forms on some tiers are processed by Formspree (their privacy policy).
  • Payment Processors — payments are handled by third-party processors. We receive payment confirmation only — never full card details.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to: deliver and manage our Services, communicate about your project, process payments, store guest form submissions on behalf of wedding clients, improve our platform, comply with legal obligations, and respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Where Your Data Is Stored

All platform data is stored in our MongoDB Atlas database cluster hosted on cloud infrastructure, with dedicated databases for each service type.

5.2 Security Measures

We implement HTTPS encryption in transit, API key authentication, rate limiting on public forms, honeypot spam protection, hashed credential storage, and access controls.

5.3 Data Retention

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With the wedding couple (our client): Guest RSVP and form data is shared with the couple for their wedding management.
  • With service providers: Hosting, payments, email, and form providers — bound by data processing obligations.
  • Legal compliance: If required by law or court order.
  • Business transfers: If Axerio Automation is acquired, data transfers under the same protections.

We do not share data with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

Our websites use cookies and similar technologies. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, port, or object to processing of your personal data. To exercise any rights, email phase-one@axerioautomation.com. We respond within 30 days.

If you are a wedding guest seeking deletion of your form submission, please contact the couple directly — they are the data controller for their site.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under 16. If we become aware that a child under 16 has submitted personal data, we will delete it promptly.
